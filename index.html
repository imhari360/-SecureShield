HAVE QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions.

Understand the basics before you contact an advisor.

Still have a question? Contact us ↗
What types of insurance can I explore?+

You can enquire about life, health, motor, home and travel insurance. Availability depends on the actual insurer and product.

How much does insurance cost?+

Premiums depend on the product, coverage, eligibility, personal details and insurer terms. Request an official quote for accurate pricing.

Can I submit an enquiry without buying a policy?+

Yes. An enquiry does not commit you to buying a policy. Review the product documents and make your own decision.

What should I check before purchasing?+

Check coverage, exclusions, waiting periods, deductibles, limits, claim conditions, premium payment terms and the insurer's official policy wording.

Does this website issue insurance policies?+

This demo website collects enquiries only. Actual policy sales, advice and issuance must be handled by appropriately authorised parties.
